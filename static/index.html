Devices

Give each instrument a short name. A device matches every MIDI port whose name contains its match text, so it is found again after you unplug it.

Routes

Tick a box to send everything from a device (row) to another device (column). Routes are wired directly by the system, so they add no delay.

Mappings

Turn one message into another: a CC into a different CC, a fader into SysEx, a SysEx message into a note, and so on. Press Learn and move the control to fill a side in.

Settings

Startup messages, network sharing and loop protection.

Startup messages

Sent to a device whenever it connects, for example to switch it to a mode. Separate messages with |.

Loop protection

Stops MIDI from going round in circles between devices and flooding them.

Network MIDI (qmidinet)

Share devices with computers on your network over UDP multicast. qmidinet must be running.
